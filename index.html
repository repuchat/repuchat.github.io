+REP and −REP among friends

REPuchat is an app for groups of friends, family, neighbours. Every day everyone has a few REPoints: give them to others as +REP or −REP and say what for. Disagree? Dispute it, and the group votes. The digest shows who stood out over the week, month, year.

Download Android Web version iPhone and computer
  • Groups by invite: a link or a QR code.
  • Reasons, places, photos, reactions, comments.
  • Disputes with a vote.
  • Digests and cards to share.
